⚔️ Tactics

This skirmish runs BG3-class tactical rules. Here's the whole toolbox.

🔥 Environmental surfaces

  • Oil Flask lays grease → Firebrand ignites it; fire spreads tile-to-tile and burns whoever stands in it.
  • Hallowed Water lays water (and douses fire → steam, which gives +2 cover). Storm Bolt arcs through water, zapping everyone wet.
  • Venom Vial → a poison cloud; Frost → ice that makes foes slip (Slowed).

🗺️ Position

  • High ground (the gilded ▲ tiles) grants advantage on your attacks; striking from below gives disadvantage. The forecast already accounts for it.
  • Leaving an adjacent foe's reach provokes an opportunity attack — spend Disengage to move safely.
  • Shield (Naeve's arcane reaction): when a foe's attack would *just* connect, she throws up a Shield for +5 AC and turns it aside — once per round, shared with opportunity attacks, gated by the ⚡ Reactions toggle.
  • Counterspell (Naeve's reaction): when an enemy casts a spell (the boss's Wail), she can spend her reaction to unravel it before it lands — it fizzles entirely. Same one-per-round reaction as Shield and opportunity attacks.
  • Concentration: a buff like Mirror Image holds only while Naeve keeps focus — take damage and she rolls a Constitution save (DC 10, or half the damage if higher) or the spell drops. Guard your concentrators.
  • Shove a foe into your fire (or into a wall for impact damage) — or off the high-ground ledge, which makes them fall (2d6 per level) and land Prone.
  • Jump (range 3) vaults over walls and units to a clear tile — and provokes no opportunity attack.
  • Throw a flask (Roen's Alchemist's Fire, range 5) onto any tile — empty ground included — to seed a hazard where you want the fight.
  • Downed & death saves — a hero at 0 HP doesn't die: they go down, rolling a death save each turn (3 ✓ stabilise, 3 ✗ die, a natural 20 revives). Spend your action on 🤝 Help beside a fallen ally to drag them up to 1 HP. The party only loses when everyone is down.

🧭 Comfort

  • Undo Move takes back a clean move · Non-lethal spares the defeated.
  • Reactions is the master switch; the ⚔ OA / 🛡 Shield / 🌀 Counter stances then hold or arm each reaction independently — keep your Shield while holding your opportunity attacks, say. A reaction fires only if the master and its stance are on.
  • Every target shows a live hit% · damage · kill% forecast; your selected hero shows incoming threat.
  • Deeds — how you win is remembered: a flawless or hard-won victory, a merciful (all spared) or ruthless (no quarter) finish, a ledge kill, the boss's spell unwoven. The Chronicle records them as story flags the wider game can read.
